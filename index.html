Our Studio Services

Custom Framing

We offer an extensive selection of premium wood, metal, and hand-finished mouldings. Our expert team utilizes archival-grade matting, UV-filtering museum glass, and reversible mounting techniques to ensure your art is beautifully presented and fully protected against environmental damage.

Fine Art Conservation

Our specialized conservators gently clean, repair, and restore aged or damaged artworks. We handle oil paintings, works on paper, and antique photographs with the utmost precision, employing museum-standard, non-invasive procedures to return pieces to their original brilliance.

Canvas Stretching & Mounting

Whether it's a freshly painted masterpiece or a delicate vintage textile, we provide professional stretching and mounting services. We utilize heavy-duty custom strainers and acid-free substrates to guarantee perfect tension and structural integrity without compromising the fabric.
